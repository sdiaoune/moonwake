# A longer postcard from the sky sea

The moon fell asleep. You brought a scarf.

You are Kip, a little moon hare with a comet-red scarf. Explore floating rooftops, enormous lotus gardens, celestial clockworks, a dreamwhale horizon, a lantern festival, a shell observatory, aurora fruit boughs, and the dawn archive. Bring the morning home.

## Follow your curiosity

The Long Dawn has **24 stages across eight worlds**. Every stage joins **three continuous sections**, with a checkpoint in each. Explore above and below the main path: roofs lead into rear galleries, pearl arches hide lower grottos, and star trails reveal sheltered return routes. Three optional seals hide in each section, nine per stage, 216 overall. None is required to finish the game. Later roof junctions connect one scenic route to another: follow the small star trails, try both directions, and use hanging lanterns to keep your dash flowing.

Move with the D-pad. Tap **A** for a short hop; hold it to reach higher. Press **B** to dash in the direction you face. Hold a direction with B to choose it immediately. Land, touch a hanging lantern, or defeat a foe to refill the dash. Stomp beetles, owls, and jelly foes, or dash through them. Thorn hazards need a careful jump. Three chained interactions restore a missing heart.

Press **Down + A** while standing on a one-way ledge to drop into the passage beneath it. Solid floors catch you normally. Moving balconies carry you as you stand on them. A wind message and drifting mote show which way the air pushes; the current affects airborne movement gently. Springs lift you higher; crumbling ledges ask you to keep moving.

## Keep a little stardust

Checkpoints refill your hearts. There is no lives counter. **Select** retries the checkpoint; collected seals and stars stay with your current run. Entering another section carries your stage clock, stars, and seals forward. **Start** pauses and offers the atlas, sound toggle, and title return. The pause screen also shows your section and active journey minutes.

Your collection, unlocked stages, current section, checkpoint, best times, and best ranks save on the cartridge. Continue resumes the saved section at its checkpoint or start. After a completed stage, Continue begins the next stage; after the final clear, it returns to the ending. The atlas lets you replay any unlocked stage from its beginning. Each atlas entry shows three seal counts, one for each section; the rightmost letter is your best stage rank.

Earn **S — Stardust** by finding all nine seals again on the current run and reaching the final gate within the displayed full-stage par. **A — Moonlit** means you found all nine beyond par; **B — Arrived** celebrates any other clear. A slower replay never takes away your best medal. Exploring without rushing is always a valid first journey.

To begin a new game, press B twice on the title. Navigation cancels the first confirmation. Original Moonwake saves migrate automatically: old unlocks and first-section discoveries carry forward, while times and medals restart for the longer stages. An old completed journey unlocks Ember Festival too. Earlier save slots remain untouched.

## Four guardians

Watch each guardian’s warm glow: a shot is coming. A jump or scarf dash can loosen a dream knot. The Rainbell Warden alternates shot heights; the Comet Manta sweeps across its roost; the Prism Sentinel’s glowing shield asks you to wait for an opening; the Dreamwhale changes its rhythm as dawn approaches. Dash through projectiles to refill your scarf. Each arena has a nearby checkpoint and broad floor for another approach.

## Play on a computer

Open the browser preview through a local HTTP server. Arrows move, Z is A, X is B, Enter is Start, and Shift is Select. Down + Z drops through a ledge. On-screen controls support simultaneous presses. The preview emulates the same native ROM.

The campaign targets a 60–90 minute first journey, with substantial optional exploration. Your pace can be faster or slower; that target is not a measured human completion time.
