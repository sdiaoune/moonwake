# A postcard from the sky sea

The moon fell asleep. You brought a scarf.

You are Kip, a little moon hare with a comet-red scarf. Follow the lanterns
over floating rooftops, enormous lotus leaves, celestial clockworks and a
dreamwhale. Bring the morning home.

## Your first leap

Move with the D-pad. Tap **A** for a short hop; hold it to reach higher.
Press **B** to dash in the direction you face. Hold a direction with B to
choose it immediately. Land, touch a hanging lantern, or defeat a foe to
refill the dash. You can stomp beetles and owls, or dash through them.

Look up. Every stage has three optional moon seals. Their little upper
routes are invitations to try a different line. Lanterns can turn a single
dash into a string of leaps; springs sing you upward; brittle ledges ask you
to keep moving. Three chained interactions restore a missing heart.

## Keep a little stardust

There are twelve stages across four worlds, with a checkpoint in each.
Checkpoints refill your hearts. There is no lives counter. **Select** retries
the checkpoint; collected seals and stars stay with your current run.
**Start** pauses and gives you the stage atlas, sound toggle and title return.

Your collection, unlocked stages, best times and best ranks save on the
cartridge. Continue starts the last stage you selected. The atlas lets you
revisit any unlocked stage.

Earn **S — Stardust** by finding all three seals again on the current run
and reaching the gate within the displayed par time. **A — Moonlit** means
you found all three beyond par; **B — Arrived** celebrates a clear with fewer
seals. A slower replay never takes away your best medal.

To begin a new game, press B twice on the title. Navigation cancels the first
confirmation, so your journey is safe from a stray button press.

## A quiet tip for the finale

Watch the dream knot's warm glow. It tells you when a shot is coming.
A jump or scarf dash can loosen the knot. Notice the broad arena and choose
your approach; the whale floats higher as the morning draws nearer.

## Play on a computer

Open the browser preview through a local HTTP server. Use arrows to move,
Z for A, X for B, Enter for Start and Shift for Select. The on-screen controls
also support simultaneous presses. The preview runs the same native ROM
installed on the cartridge.

Moonwake is a compact arcade adventure. Take a first journey, then learn the
upper routes and chase a cleaner line. The sky sea will wait.
